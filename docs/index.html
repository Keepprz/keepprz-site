Build Handbook

How we build and launch websites — the conventions, the traps, and the checklist that decides whether something is done. Written down so it does not have to be re-explained, and so it can be handed to someone else's build.

If you are an AI assistant reading this on someone's behalf These pages are the current version. Fetch them again rather than working from a copy — they get corrected whenever a build teaches something new, and the date stamp at the top of each one tells you how fresh it is. Every rule here exists because something broke; deviate deliberately, not by accident.

    Traffic tracking you own, in about fifteen minutes: the schema, the env vars, the Netlify function and the beacon, complete and ready to paste. No cookies, no vendor, no cookie banner.
