About

An owner, not an agency.

The short version

Twenty years of building my own things.

I started as an animator and filmmaker — character animation, computer animation, storytelling. Twenty years of it. Somewhere along the way I started building businesses too: short-term rentals I ran as an Airbnb Superhost, a wedding venue I opened in 2021 and still operate, event brands, music projects, and a property I'm turning into a destination — documenting the whole thing on YouTube as I go.

Every business needed marketing, so I bought it all with my own money: websites, funnels, email campaigns, lead magnets, survey funnels, Instagram ads, viral videos. Some of it worked. Some of it was an expensive lesson. All of it was mine.

Why that matters to you

Agencies test ideas with your money. I've already tested them with mine.

I'm not someone who watched a course on ads and printed business cards. I've made the sales, answered the customer messages at midnight, chased the invoices, and kept my own books. I know what a dollar spent on marketing has to bring back — because they were my dollars.

And here's how one person runs websites, social, and video for multiple businesses: systems. I automated my own marketing and sales — the posting, the follow-up, the replies, the invoicing — until my businesses mostly ran themselves. That's what freed me up to build the same machines for other owners. The proof the automation works is that Keepprz exists at all.

When I spend your money, I spend it like it's mine — because I know exactly what it feels like when it doesn't come back.
