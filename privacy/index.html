Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Keepprz builds websites, runs social media and makes custom tools for small businesses. Keepprz is operated by Jason Leue. This policy explains what information we handle on keepprz.com and through the Keepprz app on Facebook and Instagram.

What the Keepprz app does

The Keepprz app is a private business tool. We use it to publish and manage content on Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that we own, or that a client has authorized us to manage. It is not offered to the public, and it has no public sign-up.

Information we handle

Through Facebook and Instagram. For the Pages and accounts we manage, we may handle:

  • Page and account names and IDs, and the access tokens that connect them to the app
  • Posts, captions, photos and videos
  • Comments, messages and leads sent to those Pages and accounts
  • Performance numbers, such as views, reach, engagement and ad results

When you contact us. We keep what you send us, such as your name, email address, phone number and message.

When you visit keepprz.com. We count page views. We record the page, the site that referred you, your country and your device type. We also make a one-way code from your IP address and browser that changes every day, so we can count daily visitors without storing your IP address. This site's analytics use no cookies.

How we use it

  • To publish and schedule content
  • To answer comments, messages and leads
  • To measure and report results to the business that owns the account
  • To provide our services and reply to you

We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the business that owns the account and with the service providers that help us do the work. These include Meta, Netlify for website hosting, Supabase for data storage, Google for email, and Anthropic for AI tools. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information while we manage the account or provide the service, and afterward only as long as we need it for business records or legal reasons.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete information we hold about you. See our data deletion instructions. You can also remove the Keepprz app's access at any time in your Facebook settings, under Business Integrations.

Children

Our services are for businesses. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
