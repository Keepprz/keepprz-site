Data Deletion

Last updated November 18, 2026.

You can ask Keepprz to delete information we hold about you, including anything we received through the Keepprz app on Facebook or Instagram.

How to request deletion

  • Email jason@keepprz.com with the subject line "Data deletion request".
  • Tell us your name and the Facebook Page, Instagram account or email address the request is about.
  • We may ask you to confirm that the information is yours.

We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days. If the law requires us to keep a record, we will tell you what we kept and why.

Remove the app's access

To disconnect the Keepprz app from your Facebook account:

  • Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy, then Settings.
  • Open Business Integrations.
  • Find Keepprz and choose Remove.

Removing the app stops further access. To delete information we already hold, send the email above as well.
